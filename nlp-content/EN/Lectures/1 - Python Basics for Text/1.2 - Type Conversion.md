```python
# Convert explicitly when you need to mix them.
print(int("5") + 5)
print("5" + str(5))
```


**Saved output:**

```text
10
55
```

`"5" + "5"` joins two strings, giving `"55"`. Python rejects the mixed string-and-integer addition in the saved `TypeError`. Converting with `int("5")` allows numeric addition to give `10`; converting with `str(5)` allows string joining to give `"55"`. For reading such errors, see [[EN/Lectures/0 - Jupyter and Colab/0.7 - Reading Tracebacks|reading tracebacks]].



This issue appears in real data work. A rating such as `5` may arrive from a CSV or survey as the **string** `"5"`, depending on how it is read. Check the type and convert it when needed. Otherwise an operation may give a wrong answer, even when it does not raise an error.
