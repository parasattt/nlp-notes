### Strings: the type you will use most

A **string** is a piece of text. You can put it inside single or double quotes; use a consistent style.



```python
title = "The Old Man and the Sea"
author = 'Ernest Hemingway'

print(len(title))        # number of characters, spaces included
print(title.upper())
print(title.lower())
```


**Saved output:**

```text
23
THE OLD MAN AND THE SEA
the old man and the sea
```

`len(title)` counts all characters, including spaces, and gives `23`. `.upper()` and `.lower()` return versions with changed case; they do not replace `title` unless you assign the result.
