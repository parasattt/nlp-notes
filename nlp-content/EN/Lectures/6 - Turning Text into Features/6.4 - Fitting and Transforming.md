## Fit once, then transform

These methods have different jobs:

| Method | Job |
|---|---|
| `fit` | learns the vocabulary and assigns words to columns |
| `transform` | converts text using the vocabulary already learned |
| `fit_transform` | learns the vocabulary and converts the supplied text in one call |

Use `fit_transform` once on the training data. Use `transform` on test data and future messages. Fitting again can change the vocabulary and column positions, so the earlier numbers would no longer mean the same things. This distinction is essential in [[EN/Lectures/7 - Naive Bayes Classification/7.7 - Train-Test Splits and Data Leakage|the later classification workflow]].
