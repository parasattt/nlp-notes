## The problem

These four short messages contain two spam messages and two ordinary messages, labelled `ham`.



Keep the labels beside the four source messages. `ham` means an ordinary non-spam message.

```python
messages = [
    "free money now claim your prize",
    "meeting moved to three tomorrow",
    "win a free iphone click here",
    "can you send me the report before three",
]
labels = ["spam", "ham", "spam", "ham"]

for m, l in zip(messages, labels):
    print(f"{l:5} | {m}")
```


**Saved output:**

```text
spam  | free money now claim your prize
ham   | meeting moved to three tomorrow
spam  | win a free iphone click here
ham   | can you send me the report before three
```



A person can see clues: `free`, `win`, `prize` and `click` suggest spam; `meeting`, `report` and `send` suggest ordinary messages.

A mathematical model needs a fixed-length vector of numbers for each message. Every position must have the same meaning across all messages.

Before continuing, spend about thirty seconds choosing what those numbers could represent. Try to invent a representation yourself; this leads naturally to bag of words.
