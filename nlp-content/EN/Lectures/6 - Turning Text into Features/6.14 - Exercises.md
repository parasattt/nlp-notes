## Exercises

**1. Fix the vocabulary for a Kazakh corpus.**

```python
kk_corpus = [
    "мен бүгін кітап оқыдым",
    "ол кеше мектепке барды",
    "біз дүкеннен нан алдық",
    "сен кітапханада кітап оқыдың",
    "олар үйде фильм көрді",
]
```

Vectorize this corpus with each of the following settings and report all three vocabulary sizes:

1. `token_pattern=r"[a-zA-Z]+"`
2. the default settings
3. the default settings with `min_df=2`

For the third version, list the surviving words and explain their shared property. With only five documents, does `min_df=2` mainly remove noise or useful information? Support your answer with the actual words.

---

**2. Does normalisation shrink the vocabulary, and what does it cost?**

Vectorize the review corpus twice: first as raw text, then after lemmatizing every token with `WordNetLemmatizer`. Remember its POS argument.

```python
raw_reviews = [
    "the batteries were dying and the screen was cracking",
    "the battery died and the screen cracked",
    "it studies my habits and studied them well",
    "this study of the product was thorough",
]
```

Report both vocabulary sizes and the percentage decrease. Find at least one pair with different meanings that lemmatization incorrectly merges into one feature. The model can no longer distinguish those words; examine `study` as a possible example.

---

**3. Beat the negation trap under a budget.**

Using the earlier four-review `reviews` list, try combinations of `ngram_range`, `min_df` and `stop_words`. Find settings meeting all three conditions:

- `not good` (or another negation bigram present in the corpus) survives as a feature
- the total vocabulary is **smaller** than plain `ngram_range=(1, 2)` with no trimming
- no single-document typo-like token survives

Record the final settings and the vocabulary size for every attempt. Explain which information your choices remove in exchange for meeting the budget. Naming that trade is part of the exercise.
