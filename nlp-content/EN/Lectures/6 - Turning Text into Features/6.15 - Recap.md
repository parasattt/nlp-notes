## Recap

- A model needs text represented by a fixed-size vector of numbers.
- **Bag of words** uses one column per vocabulary word and stores its count. `CountVectorizer` automates the hand-built version.
- `fit` learns the vocabulary; `transform` applies it. Fit on training data once.
- Document-word matrices are mostly zeros, so use **sparse matrices**. Avoid expanding a real corpus with `.toarray()`.
- Every default can remove information: `lowercase` removes capitalisation, `token_pattern` drops one-letter words and `stop_words="english"` removes `not`.
- Stop word removal can turn opposite reviews into identical vectors. Check the representation before training.
- **Bigrams** add phrase features, including negation phrases, but grow the vocabulary. `min_df` controls growth and can also remove useful rare phrases.
- Bag of words gives identical vectors to `dog bites man` and `man bites dog`. It is useful when word evidence is enough; tasks needing order motivate richer representations.
