Always saying "3 stars" is off by about 1.17 stars on average. That is the number to beat.

### Four features we choose ourselves

With several features, the line becomes a **weighted sum**: one weight per feature, plus the bias.

$$\hat{y} = w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + w_3 x_3 + w_4 x_4 + b$$

Multiplying matching numbers of two vectors and adding the products up is called the **dot
product**, written $w \cdot x$. So the model is $\hat{y} = w \cdot x + b$ - the same formula as the
line, with vectors instead of single numbers. Our features:

| feature | meaning |
|---|---|
| $x_1$ | number of words from a short positive list (`great`, `delicious`, `friendly`, ...) |
| $x_2$ | number of words from a short negative list (`worst`, `rude`, `never`, ...) |
| $x_3$ | length of the review, in hundreds of words |
| $x_4$ | number of `!` |

```python
POSITIVE = ["great", "good", "amazing", "love", "best", "delicious", "friendly", "excellent", "awesome", "perfect"]
NEGATIVE = ["bad", "worst", "terrible", "rude", "horrible", "never", "disappointed", "awful", "slow", "not"]

def review_features(text):
    words = re.findall(r"[a-z']+", text.lower())
    return [sum(w in POSITIVE for w in words),
            sum(w in NEGATIVE for w in words),
            len(words) / 100,
            text.count("!")]

F_train = np.array([review_features(t) for t in y_train_df["text"]])
F_test = np.array([review_features(t) for t in y_test_df["text"]])
print("F_train shape:", F_train.shape)

four = LinearRegression().fit(F_train, y_train_df["stars"])
for name, w in zip(["positive words", "negative words", "length (100 words)", "exclamation marks"], four.coef_):
    print(f"weight of {name:20}: {w:+.3f}")
print(f"bias                          : {four.intercept_:+.3f}")
print()
print(f"test MAE: {mae(four.predict(F_test), y_test_df['stars']):.3f}  (baseline 1.173)")
```

**Saved output:**

```text
F_train shape: (12000, 4)
weight of positive words      : +0.311
weight of negative words      : -0.279
weight of length (100 words)  : -0.100
weight of exclamation marks   : +0.046
bias                          : +2.965

test MAE: 0.973  (baseline 1.173)
```

Every learned weight can be read in stars:

- each **positive word** adds about 0.31 stars, each **negative word** subtracts about 0.28
- every **100 extra words** subtract about 0.1 stars: unhappy customers tend to write longer reviews
- each **`!`** adds a little: 0.05 stars
- the **bias**, 2.97, is the prediction for a short review with none of these features

Four features already beat the baseline: 0.97 instead of 1.17. Compute one prediction by hand - a
review with 3 positive words, 1 negative word, 150 words and 2 exclamation marks:

$$0.311 \cdot 3 - 0.279 \cdot 1 - 0.100 \cdot 1.5 + 0.046 \cdot 2 + 2.965 \approx 3.56 \text{ stars}$$

```python
x_one = np.array([3, 1, 1.5, 2])
print("by hand  :", round(np.dot(four.coef_, x_one) + four.intercept_, 2))
print("sklearn  :", round(four.predict([x_one])[0], 2))
```

**Saved output:**

```text
by hand  : 3.56
sklearn  : 3.56
```
