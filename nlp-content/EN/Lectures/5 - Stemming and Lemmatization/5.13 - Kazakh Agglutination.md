## Kazakh suffix chains

Kazakh is **agglutinative**: grammatical information is added through a chain of suffixes attached to a root.



Read the growing suffix chain from the base `кітап` to the source translation “in our books”.

```python
print("кітап            = book")
print("кітаптар         = book + PLURAL")
print("кітаптарым       = book + PLURAL + my")
print("кітаптарымыз     = book + PLURAL + our")
print("кітаптарымызда   = book + PLURAL + our + in   ('in our books')")
```


**Saved output:**

```text
кітап            = book
кітаптар         = book + PLURAL
кітаптарым       = book + PLURAL + my
кітаптарымыз     = book + PLURAL + our
кітаптарымызда   = book + PLURAL + our + in   ('in our books')
```



The suffixes can combine, so the lecture describes hundreds of possible forms from one root. It argues that listing every form in a dictionary is not practical for this exercise.
