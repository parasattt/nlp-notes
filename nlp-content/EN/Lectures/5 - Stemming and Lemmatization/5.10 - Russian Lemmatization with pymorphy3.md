## Lemmatizing with pymorphy3

The lecture presents `pymorphy3` as a dictionary-based tool with several million word forms. It returns a lemma and grammatical information.

`morph.parse(w)[0]` chooses the first ranked analysis. `normal_form` gives its lemma, and `tag.POS` supplies its POS label.

```python
import pymorphy3

morph = pymorphy3.MorphAnalyzer()

for w in ["ноутбука", "хорошего", "бежавшего", "покупкам", "работали"]:
    p = morph.parse(w)[0]
    print(f"{w:12} -> {p.normal_form:12} ({p.tag.POS})")
```


**Saved output:**

```text
ноутбука     -> ноутбук      (NOUN)
хорошего     -> хороший      (ADJF)
бежавшего    -> бежать       (PRTF)
покупкам     -> покупка      (NOUN)
работали     -> работать     (VERB)
```



The output contains full word forms and POS labels. The example `бежавшего -> бежать` shows a change that the illustrated suffix-removal stemmer does not recover.
