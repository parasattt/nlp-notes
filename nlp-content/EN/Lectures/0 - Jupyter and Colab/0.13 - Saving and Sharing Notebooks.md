### Saving your work

Colab autosaves notebooks to Google Drive. It is still useful to build the habit of pressing **Ctrl + S**.

- **File → Save a copy in Drive** creates your own editable copy. Do this when a teacher shares a notebook so that your edits belong to a copy you can keep.
- **File → Download → .ipynb** saves a notebook file on your computer. This is the file to submit.
