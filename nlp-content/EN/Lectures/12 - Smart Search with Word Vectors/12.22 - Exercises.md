## Exercises

**1. Your own catalogue.**

1. Compute by hand the cosine of $a = (1, 2)$ and $b = (2, 1)$, then check with `cosine`.
2. Add ten products of your own to `catalogue` (lamps, beds, wardrobes...) and five queries that
   `keyword_search` cannot find.
3. Run them through `SynonymSearch`. Which ones are fixed? Which wrong products appear? Find a
   threshold and a block list that work for all five.

---

**2. More checks.**

1. Add six checks of your own to `CHECKS`: two pairs that must be close, two that must be far, two
   analogies (for example singular and plural: `car : cars = apple : ?`).
2. Run `run_checks` on GloVe and on the Yelp model. Explain each failure in one sentence.
3. Train two more Yelp models with `window=2` and `window=10`, and run the checks on them. People
   often say that small windows find words that can *replace* each other, and large windows find
   words about the same *topic*. Do your results agree?

---

**3. Search in Kazakh.**

1. Load the Kazakh reviews of KazSAnDRA as in the week 5 optional notebook
   (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IS2AI/KazSAnDRA/main/dataset/01_pc_train_ib.zip`), cut the
   `text` column into lowercase words with `re.findall(r"\w+", ...)`, train Word2Vec and save the
   vectors.
2. Build a `SynonymSearch` with your vectors and a small Kazakh catalogue (change `words_of` so it
   keeps Kazakh letters). Show three queries where it helps.
3. In Kazakh one root has many forms (*жақсы*, *жақсылап*, ...), and each form gets its own vector.
   Compare the vocabulary size with `min_count=5` and `min_count=2`, and suggest one way to reduce
   the problem (hint: weeks 2 and 3).
