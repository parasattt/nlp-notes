### Compare directions rather than sizes

To understand word vectors, first use made-up two-number vectors. Real vectors will be learned later.

| Word | First number | Second number |
|---|---|---|
| sofa | 0.9 | 0.2 |
| couch | 0.8 | 0.3 |
| banana | 0.1 | 0.9 |

Each vector is an arrow from the origin. `sofa` and `couch` point in almost the same direction; `banana` points elsewhere. **Cosine similarity** turns this comparison into a number:

$$\cos(a, b) = \frac{a \cdot b}{|a| \cdot |b|}$$

Here, $a$ and $b$ are the two vectors.

1. Find the **dot product** $a \cdot b$: multiply values in matching positions, then add them.
2. Find each vector's **length**, written as $|a|$ for vector $a$: square its values, add them, and take the square root.
3. Divide the dot product by the product of the lengths. This removes the effect of vector size, leaving a comparison of direction.

A cosine of 1 means the arrows point the same way; 0 means they are at right angles; −1 means they point in opposite directions. The lecture describes 0 as having nothing in common in this representation.

#### Sofa and couch, by hand

- Dot product: $0.9 \cdot 0.8 + 0.2 \cdot 0.3 = 0.72 + 0.06 = 0.78$.
- Lengths: $|\text{sofa}| = \sqrt{0.81 + 0.04} = 0.922$, $\;|\text{couch}| = \sqrt{0.64 + 0.09} = 0.854$.
- Cosine: $0.78 / (0.922 \cdot 0.854) = 0.78 / 0.787 \approx 0.99$.

The result is close to 1, so these vectors point in almost the same direction.

#### Sofa and banana, by hand

The dot product is $0.9 \cdot 0.1 + 0.2 \cdot 0.9 = 0.27$. Banana's length is $\sqrt{0.01 + 0.81} = 0.906$. Therefore the cosine is $0.27 / (0.922 \cdot 0.906) \approx 0.32$, much lower than for sofa/couch.

In NumPy, `a @ b` calculates the dot product and `np.linalg.norm(a)` calculates a vector's length.



```python
def cosine(a, b):
    return a @ b / (np.linalg.norm(a) * np.linalg.norm(b))

sofa, couch, banana = np.array([0.9, 0.2]), np.array([0.8, 0.3]), np.array([0.1, 0.9])
print(f"cosine(sofa, couch)  = {cosine(sofa, couch):.2f}")
print(f"cosine(sofa, banana) = {cosine(sofa, banana):.2f}")
```


**Saved output:**

```text
cosine(sofa, couch)  = 0.99
cosine(sofa, banana) = 0.32
```
