### Step 3: include fake pairs

If the model saw only real pairs, it could answer “yes” every time. To give it a comparison, add three **fake pairs** for every real one. Keep the center word, but choose random neighbor words—for example, (`pizza`, `rude`).

The target answer is 1 for a real pair and 0 for a fake one. This process is **negative sampling**. In the source example, common words such as *the* are sampled as fake neighbors more often; the lecture uses this to explain how their influence is reduced.
