### The search problem

A furniture shop in Almaty receives this complaint:

> “I searched for **sofa** and got nothing.”

The opening story says the shop sells eleven sofas, but its pages call them **couches**. Exact word matching treats `sofa` and `couch` as different, so it misses the products.

The proposed fix is a **word vector**: a list of numbers for each word. If words used in similar ways get similar vectors, the program can compare their numbers instead of relying only on identical spellings.

The lecture follows these steps:

1. Reproduce the search failure.
2. Measure similarity between two vectors, including a calculation by hand.
3. Count neighboring words to see where useful vectors can come from.
4. Learn vectors with Word2Vec.
5. Train on about 426,000 sentences from Yelp reviews.
6. Load a model trained on six billion words and test its limits.
7. Use the vectors to improve the search.
