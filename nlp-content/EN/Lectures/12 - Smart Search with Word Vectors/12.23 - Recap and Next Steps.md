### Recap

- Exact matching cannot find synonyms. Distinct one-hot word vectors have cosine similarity 0.
- Cosine similarity is $\cos(a, b) = \dfrac{a \cdot b}{|a| \cdot |b|}$. A value of 1 means the same direction; 0 means a right angle.
- Word vectors store a small list of numbers—100 per word in the larger example. Similar neighboring words can lead to similar vectors.
- Word2Vec learns by separating real center/neighbor pairs from fake ones. It scores pairs with $\sigma(v \cdot u)$ and updates vectors with $v \leftarrow v - \eta (p - y) u$.
- Gensim provides training and the `most_similar`, `similarity`, and `doesnt_match` operations. Check vocabulary membership before a lookup.
- Pretrained GloVe is useful but can put opposites close together, reflect old facts, mix word senses, and repeat bias from its texts.
- Query expansion combines nearby words with a threshold, a block list, and a cache.
