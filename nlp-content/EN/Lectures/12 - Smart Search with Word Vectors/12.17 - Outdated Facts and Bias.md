### Old information and learned bias

The third saved analogy returns **Almaty** for Kazakhstan's capital. The lecture explains that Almaty was the capital until 1997 and remained strongly associated with Kazakhstan in the model's training texts. A vector model reflects what its texts say; it is not a check of present-day facts.

The fourth analogy returns **nurse** from the man/doctor and woman relationship. This repeats associations in the training texts. The source calls this **bias**, citing Bolukbasi and colleagues (2016). It warns that using such vectors to sort job applications could treat men and women differently for reasons unrelated to an applicant's qualifications.
