### A word outside the vocabulary

The trained model kept only words seen at least five times, according to `min_count`. A typo such as `sofaaa` may not be present. Calling a vector lookup for an unknown word raises an error, shown in the saved example below.



```python
query = "sofaaa"
try:
    yelp_vectors.most_similar(query)
except KeyError as e:
    print("KeyError:", e)

print(query in yelp_vectors, "pizza" in yelp_vectors)     # check first, then ask
```


**Saved output:**

```text
KeyError: "Key 'sofaaa' not present in vocabulary"
False True
```

The vocabulary issue is related to [[EN/Lectures/4 - Tokenization/4.9 - Unknown Words and Subword Tokenization|unknown words and subword tokenization]], which uses a different way to represent unseen whole words.



Check `word in vectors` before looking up its neighbors. For an unknown word, the search can fall back to the original word without expansion.
