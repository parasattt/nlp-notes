### Represent rare and unseen words

Names and typos in the test reviews may never have appeared in training. Build a fixed vocabulary from words seen **at least twice**. Replace other words with `<unk>`, meaning “unknown.”

Training examples of this marker let the model learn where rare words occur. At test time, mapping unseen words to the same marker avoids requiring a separate count for every new spelling.



`vocab_yelp` is learned from training word frequencies. `prepare` maps other words to `<unk>` and adds boundary markers to both train and test sentences.

```python
word_counts = Counter(w for s in train_sents for w in s)
vocab_yelp = {w for w, c in word_counts.items() if c >= 2}
print(f"different words in training: {len(word_counts):,}   kept (seen 2+ times): {len(vocab_yelp):,}")

def prepare(sent):
    return ["<s>"] + [w if w in vocab_yelp else "<unk>" for w in sent] + ["</s>"]

train_p = [prepare(s) for s in train_sents]
test_p = [prepare(s) for s in test_sents]
print(" ".join(test_p[3]))
```


**Saved output:**

```text
different words in training: 63,279   kept (seen 2+ times): 35,637
```


**Saved output:**

```text
<s> they are closed certain days and then open at noon on certain days </s>
```
