### Predict the next word

A support team answers 400 emails each day, and many replies start in the same way. While an agent types *thank you for your*, a helper should suggest words such as **order**, **patience**, or **feedback**, like a phone keyboard.

A **language model** gives probabilities for the next word based on the words already written. The lecture mentions ChatGPT as a much larger example, then builds a simple count-based model: first from five replies that can be checked on paper, then from about 426,000 real-review sentences.

The learning sequence is:

1. Count which words follow other words.
2. Calculate the probability of a whole sentence.
3. Handle unseen pairs and products too small for the computer to store.
4. Scale the method to Yelp, including rare words, fallback, memory, and speed.
5. Evaluate it with perplexity.
6. Generate text.
7. Explain what counting misses and connect that limitation to word vectors.

The calculations use fractions and logarithms, with new terms explained as they appear.
