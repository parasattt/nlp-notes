### Combine the steps of a reply

For *thank you for contacting us*, calculate each next-word probability, including the sentence boundaries:

| Step | Probability |
|---|---|
| $P(\text{thank} \mid \text{<s>})$ | 3/5 |
| $P(\text{you} \mid \text{thank})$ | 3/3 |
| $P(\text{for} \mid \text{you})$ | 3/3 |
| $P(\text{contacting} \mid \text{for})$ | 1/3 |
| $P(\text{us} \mid \text{contacting})$ | 1/1 |
| $P(\text{</s>} \mid \text{us})$ | 1/1 |
| **Product** | $0.6 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 \cdot 0.333 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 0.2$ |

Multiply because the reply requires these steps to happen in sequence. The lecture compares this with two coin heads: $0.5\cdot0.5=0.25$.

For a general word sequence, the chain rule is:

$$P(w_1 w_2 \dots w_n) = P(w_1) \cdot P(w_2 \mid w_1) \cdot P(w_3 \mid w_1 w_2) \cdots$$

$w_i$ is the word at position $i$. Each factor asks for that word's probability given the words before it.
