### Suggest the highest-probability words

Look up words that followed the last typed word and rank them by their counts. The next functions calculate a probability and return up to `k=3` suggestions.



`p_next` divides a following-word count by the total for that previous word, returning 0 if no row was observed. `suggest_toy` returns the `k` most common continuations and rounds their probabilities for display.

```python
def p_next(word, prev):
    total = sum(follows[prev].values())
    return follows[prev][word] / total if total else 0.0

def suggest_toy(prev, k=3):
    return [(w, round(p_next(w, prev), 2)) for w, _ in follows[prev].most_common(k)]

print("after 'your' :", suggest_toy("your"))
print("after 'order':", suggest_toy("order"))
```


**Saved output:**

```text
after 'your' : [('order', 0.75), ('patience', 0.25)]
after 'order': [('</s>', 0.33), ('has', 0.33), ('is', 0.33)]
```



A `</s>` suggestion after `order` means the reply may end there. The lecture says an editor could show this as a full stop.
