### One unseen pair can make a sentence score zero

Consider *your order is shipped*. It is normal English, but the five training replies never contain the pair *is shipped*. The next example checks its score.



```python
print(sentence_prob("your order is shipped"))
print("count of 'is shipped':", follows["is"]["shipped"])
```


**Saved output:**

```text
0.0
count of 'is shipped': 0
```



The saved sentence probability is **zero** because $P(\text{shipped} \mid \text{is})=0/1=0$. A single zero factor makes the entire product zero.

The model is treating “not observed in this small dataset” as “impossible.” Real data also contains many possible word pairs that never appear in training. Later evaluation measures how often this happens.
