## Recap

- A **token** is the unit you choose to treat as indivisible. It depends on the task.
- `.split()` keeps punctuation attached, separating counts that should often be combined.
- The lecture uses `word_tokenize` as a general starting point, `TweetTokenizer` for social text and `wordpunct_tokenize` for stronger splitting. Compare their actual results on your data.
- `sent_tokenize` uses a trained model and handles the example abbreviations `Dr.` and `p.m.`.
- **Stop word** removal can sharpen topic counts and damage sentiment. Protect negations.
- `[a-zA-Z]+` silently returns no tokens for Cyrillic text. Use a Unicode-aware pattern such as `\w+` or a suitable tokenizer.
- **Subword tokenization** addresses the unknown-word problem by representing words as pieces. The saved Kazakh examples also show that pieces need not be morphemes.
- Normalization lets a search for `battery draining` find `Batteries drain`.
