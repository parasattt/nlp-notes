**Saved output:**

```text
                                                     review                                                     clean
0  Excellent laptop, fast and quiet. Battery lasts all day.  excellent laptop, fast and quiet. battery lasts all day.
1     Screen died after three weeks. Support was unhelpful.     screen died after three weeks. support was unhelpful.
2               Good phone for the price, camera is decent.               good phone for the price, camera is decent.
```

The chained operations lowercase each review and remove whitespace at its ends. The result goes into `clean`, while the original remains in `review` for checking and recovery.



Keep the original text and write the cleaned version in a **new column**. Cleaning changes the data, and your first rules may be wrong. If you overwrite the original, you may need to reload the file to recover it.
