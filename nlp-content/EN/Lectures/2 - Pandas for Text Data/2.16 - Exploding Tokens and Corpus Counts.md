`.explode()` turns a list-valued cell into separate rows, one row for each item. After `.str.split()`, the twelve review rows hold lists of words; exploding them produces a single column of individual tokens suitable for `Counter`.

The saved twelve most common tokens include `the`, `and`, and `is`, which often say little about the topic. Punctuation also remains attached, as in `laptop,` and `quiet.`. The source additionally mentions `it.` and `money.`; those tokens occur in the corpus but are **not in the displayed top-twelve result**.
