### Feature Matrix and Labels

Матрица признаков и ответы

```python
tickets = pd.DataFrame({
    "ticket": ["Where is my order???",
               "Charged twice, refund NOW!!!",
               "Thanks, all good",
               "The screen arrived cracked, I want a refund"],
    "minutes_to_resolve": [6, 25, 1, 30],
})

def ticket_features(text):
    words = re.findall(r"[a-z]+", text.lower())
    return [len(words), text.count("!") + text.count("?"), int("refund" in words)]

X_small = np.array([ticket_features(t) for t in tickets["ticket"]])
y_small = tickets["minutes_to_resolve"].to_numpy()

print("feature columns: [number of words, number of ! and ?, mentions refund]")
print("X =\n", X_small)
print("y =", y_small)
print("shape of X:", X_small.shape, "  shape of y:", y_small.shape)
```
