### Imbalanced Classes

Несбалансированные классы

```python
truth = np.array([1] * 50 + [0] * 950)
lazy = np.zeros(1000, dtype=int)
print(f"accuracy  {accuracy_score(truth, lazy):.3f}")
print(f"recall    {recall_score(truth, lazy):.3f}")
print(f"F1        {f1_score(truth, lazy, zero_division=0):.3f}")
```
