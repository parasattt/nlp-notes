### Выбор столбцов

Выберите один столбец по имени с помощью `df["review"]`. Чтобы выбрать несколько, передайте список имён, как в следующих ячейках.



```python
df["review"]              # one column -> a Series
```


**Сохранённый вывод:**

```text
0     Excellent laptop, fast and quiet. Battery lasts all day.
1        Screen died after three weeks. Support was unhelpful.
2                  Good phone for the price, camera is decent.
3               Battery drains far too quickly, disappointing.
4            Perfect for reading and taking notes. Very light.
5                   Does the job but the keyboard feels cheap.
6       Best phone I have owned. Fast, great screen, worth it.
7                      Stopped charging within a month. Avoid.
8           Nice tablet overall, though the speakers are weak.
9          Overheats under any real load. Not worth the money.
10              It is fine. Nothing special, nothing terrible.
11       Fantastic build quality and the display is beautiful.
Name: review, dtype: str
```



```python
df[["product", "rating"]] # several columns -> a DataFrame. Note the DOUBLE brackets.
```


**Сохранённый вывод:**

```text
   product  rating
0   laptop       5
1   laptop       1
2    phone       4
3    phone       2
4   tablet       5
5   laptop       3
6    phone       5
7   tablet       1
8   tablet       4
9   laptop       2
10   phone       3
11  tablet       5
```



У двойных скобок две задачи: внешние выбирают данные из DataFrame, а внутренние создают **список имён столбцов**.

- Одно имя, например `df["review"]`, возвращает **Series**.
- Список имён, например `df[["product", "rating"]]`, возвращает **DataFrame**.
